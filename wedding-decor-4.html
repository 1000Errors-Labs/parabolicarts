Wedding décor

Designed With Intention, Crafted With Care, Made For Your Day

Designed with intention,

Crafted with care, 

Made for your day.

I create exquisite sculptural décor, specially crafted for couples seeking pieces that are personal, expressive, and thoughtfully designed with great care. My work includes fully bespoke commissions tailored to your unique story, along with a carefully curated selection of existing designs that reflect a refined aesthetic.

BESPOKE COMMISSION

A bespoke piece is created entirely for your day, shaped with care from the first spark of an idea to the moment it stands in your space.

We begin with the stories and details that matter most to you, including any symbols, numbers, initials, or motifs that hold meaning.
I translate these into a refined sculptural language, shaping ideas and guiding the design until every line and contour feels beautifully in tune with your vision.

The finished piece is crafted with quiet precision. It is made to feel personal, expressive, and deeply connected to the character of your day.

Curated Collection

These are select designs from my studio, available to hire or adapt. Each piece carries the same craftsmanship, presence, and sculptural detail as a bespoke artwork, but offers a faster and more accessible way to bring something refined into your celebration.

Every design has been considered for beauty, atmosphere, and how it lives within a space. You can choose a piece exactly as it is, or have it tailored with subtle changes in scale, colour, or lighting so it feels completely at home in your setting.

It’s a way to bring signature, handcrafted décor into your day without the longer timelines of a full commission, while still keeping the work personal, expressive, and thoughtfully made.

ELEVATED ENHANCEMENTS

A collection of thoughtful enhancements is available to deepen the atmosphere of your celebration and bring an added sense of refinement to your décor. Soft ambient LED lighting can introduce a warm, welcoming glow that shifts gently with the mood of the day. Laser engraved name plates offer an elegant way to guide guests while keeping the aesthetic cohesive and sculptural.

For a more immersive experience, projection mapped animations can bring subtle movement and visual depth to your setting. I can also create custom visual pieces from your own photographs, shaping them into an artistic story that feels personal, expressive, and beautifully attuned to your celebration.

These enhancements enrich the presence of your décor and help create moments that feel vivid, memorable, and deeply atmospheric.

FOR THE BRIDE AND GROOM

If you are planning your wedding and want artwork that feels personal to you both, I create sculptural pieces shaped around your story, your symbolism, and the atmosphere you want to bring into your day. Each commission is made with care so it becomes something meaningful that is yours.

Whether you would like to hire a statement piece or commission a bespoke artwork for your ceremony or reception, I can design and build something that feels at home within your venue while honouring your style as a couple.

Every commission is guided with care from the first idea to the final installation. I take time to understand your theme, your colour palette, the energy you want the space to hold, and the feeling you want your guests to remember. The final piece feels intentional and expressive, and is thoughtfully integrated into the heart of your celebration.

FOR WEDDING PLANNERS

I work with wedding planners who want sculptural décor that brings something refined and expressive into their couples’ celebrations. Whether you need a ceremony focal point, a reception feature, or a custom piece for your brief, I design work that supports your vision and settles naturally into the atmosphere you are creating.

My approach is calm, clear, and guided by careful detail, and I adapt easily to different venues, budgets, and planning styles. Each piece is crafted to feel intentional and cohesive, aligned with the experience you want your clients to have, bringing a considered sense of luxury to your décor.

If you want a more distinctive and art driven environment, I can help you shape ideas, refine mood boards, or create visuals for your presentation. My aim is to keep the process smooth and enjoyable while giving you a piece that settles beautifully into the final design.
