SCREENS & ROOM DIVIDERS

SCREENS & ROOM DIVIDERS

A bespoke screen or room divider defines a space without closing it off, filtering light and casting layered shadows that shift through the day.

Each design is drawn around your room: its proportions, its materials and the way light moves through it. Patterns can draw on sacred geometry, Islamic art and church window design, or on motifs that carry meaning for you, built up in layers for depth and made to the exact dimensions of the space.

Pieces can stand freely, fit between walls or hang from the ceiling, with coloured inlays where you want a touch of extra warmth.

Business & Hospitality

Business & Hospitality

For bars, restaurants, hotels, retreats and wellness spaces, a sculptural installation can become the signature of the room: the detail guests remember and photograph.

I create feature screens, partitions and wall installations designed around your brand and your space, along with laser engraved details such as signage and name plates that keep the look cohesive throughout.

Every piece is engineered for straightforward installation, planned around your opening hours, and built to stand up to everyday use.

HOW IT WORKS

HOW IT WORKS

Every project begins with a conversation about the space, how it is used and the feeling you want it to hold. Photos, measurements and plans all help.

I design each piece in my own software, so you can see it in 3D, in place and to scale, before anything is cut. Pattern, proportion and layout are easy to refine at this stage.

Once the design is approved, every panel is laser cut, layered and finished in my Bristol studio, then delivered and installed. Larger pieces are designed to pack flat and assemble on site, so they can travel anywhere in the UK and beyond.
