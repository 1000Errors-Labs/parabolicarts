Geometric archway

A ceremonial gateway crafted for the special day

Designed for the couple’s ceremony, this archway was built from intricately layered wooden forms, bringing depth, structure, and gentle light into the space. Its sculptural presence shaped the atmosphere and offered a refined centrepiece for the celebration.

The archway was designed to bring a sculptural presence to the ceremony, using layered geometric shapes to create depth and quiet movement.

The intention was to form a gateway that felt expressive yet calm, offering a moment of arrival that supported the atmosphere of the day.

The archway was built from intricately layered wooden forms, each piece designed to nest with precision and create a sense of depth and movement.

The structure balances strength with lightness, allowing the geometry to feel expressive without appearing heavy.

. Warm illumination was integrated to enhance the contours of the design and bring a gentle clarity to the ceremony space.

The archway became a sculptural focal point for the ceremony, offering a warm sense of presence that supported the atmosphere of the day.

Its layered geometry framed the moment with clarity and intention, creating a setting that felt both expressive and calm.

As guests moved through the space, the piece provided a gentle point of focus that shaped the flow and rhythm of the celebration.

The archway settled into the space with a quiet sense of clarity, offering a sculptural presence that felt both refined and expressive.

Its layered geometry and gentle illumination shaped the atmosphere of the day and provided a meaningful focal point for the celebration.

If you are planning a wedding and want sculptural décor that shapes the room with warmth and presence, I would love to hear from you. Tell me about your venue and the feeling you want your guests to experience, and I will create a bespoke installation that fits naturally into your day.
